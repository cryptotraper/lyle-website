Flock Consulting presents

Save together. Fly together.

In development · wireframes

Flyy turns the pardner, susu or tanda your family has always trusted into an app. Friends and community groups save on a schedule and take turns collecting the pot. Nobody has to hold the cash: members e-transfer each other directly, and Flyy never holds or moves anyone's money. Flyy does not guarantee payouts.

How Flyy works

Start a flock. Everyone pays in. Everyone gets a turn to fly.

01

Start a flock

Invite your people and set the amount and the schedule.

02

Everyone pays in

Members e-transfer each other as they always have. The payer taps “I sent it” and the recipient taps “Received.”

03

Take your turn

Each round, one member collects their hand: the whole pot, sent directly by the others.

04

Keep the record

A downloadable savings history of every contribution, to show a lender.

These are low-fidelity wireframes: they show layout, content and flow, not the final visual design. Names and amounts are sample placeholders. Open banking and credit reporting are planned for a later version.

Flow 1 · Onboarding

From invite link to verified member.

Most people arrive from an organizer's invite. They sign up with their phone, verify their ID, and land in the flock that invited them.

Flow 2 · Start a flock

An organizer sets up the circle in three steps.

On a wide screen the setup is a wizard with a live summary beside it, so the organizer always sees the pot, the schedule and who's in.

Flow 3 · Each round

Pay in, collect, confirm.

The core loop every member repeats. The dashboard always leads with the next thing to do, and the flock page shows everyone who's paid.

Flow 4 · On track and on record

The app chases payments, and the record follows you.

Organizers stop chasing payments by hand, problems get resolved in the open, and every member builds a savings history that savings circles have never produced.
