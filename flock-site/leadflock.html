The problem

Weeks lost to lists

Most pre-seed founders build investor lists by hand, and most of those investors are a bad fit for their stage, sector or cheque size.

Best leads burned

Generic cold emails get ignored, and founders waste their best leads on weak first messages.

Time away from the business

Fundraising pulls the founder away from building the company.

The insight

Nobody invests until someone leads.

Founders with real customers and real communities stall because they can't find the first 20% of the round. Find the leads, and the flock follows.

The solution

An investor pipeline, built daily, sent by you.

01

Find

Angels, angel groups, pre-seed funds and community investors who write $10K–$50K cheques.

02

Score

Sector, stage, cheque size, community ties and warm-intro paths. Warmest first.

03

Reason

One line per investor on why you're relevant, from their public activity.

04

Draft

A short, personal email in your voice. You edit it and you send it.

How it runs

From intake to warm meetings in weeks, not months.

STEP 1

Intake

A kickoff call on your story, your round, your network and your writing voice.

STEP 2

Daily batch

Up to 20 new investors a day at first, then about 10 well-qualified ones.

STEP 3

You send

From your inbox, in your words, with an opt-out line. Two follow-up templates included.

STEP 4

Weekly summary

The warmest prospects, the intros worth asking for, and sharper targeting based on who's replying.
