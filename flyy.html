Flock Consulting presents

Save together. Fly together.

In development · wireframes

Flyy turns the pardner, susu or tanda your family has always trusted into an app. Friends and community groups save on a schedule and take turns collecting the pot. Nobody has to hold the cash: members e-transfer each other directly, and Flyy never holds or moves anyone's money.

How Flyy works

Start a flock. Everyone pays in. Everyone gets a turn to fly.

01

Start a flock

Invite your people and set the amount and the schedule.

02

Everyone pays in

Members e-transfer each other as they always have. Flyy tracks who has paid.

03

Take your turn

Each round, one member collects the whole pot, sent directly by the others.

04

Keep the record

A history of every contribution, ready to show a lender.

These are low-fidelity wireframes: they show layout, content and flow, not the final visual design. Names and amounts are sample placeholders.

Flow 1 · Onboarding

From invite link to verified member.

Most people arrive from a lead bird's invite. They sign up with their phone, verify their ID, and land in the flock that invited them.

Flow 2 · Start a flock

A lead bird sets up the circle in three steps.

On a wide screen the setup is a wizard with a live summary beside it, so the lead bird always sees the pot, the schedule and who's in.

Flow 3 · Each round

Pay in, collect, confirm.

The core loop every member repeats. The dashboard always leads with the next thing to do, and the flock page shows everyone who's paid.

Flow 4 · On track and on record

The app chases payments, and the record follows you.

Lead birds stop chasing payments by hand, and every member builds the documentation lenders ask for and savings circles have never produced.
